[new]

Portals have a new look, designed to make managing guest allocations feel simpler from the first invitation through to arrival.

## Everything in one place

The new home gives you an immediate view of allocation usage, invitations, and confirmed guests. Each allocation shows its date and remaining capacity alongside the actions you need most: invite guests or copy a booking link to share.

![Meridian Gallery portal showing guest allocations for a preview, artist talk, and complimentary public day tickets](assets/2026-07-29-portals/meridian-portal-allocations.png)

## A guest list that stays organised

Everyone across your allocations now appears in one guest list. Search by name or email, filter by allocation, and switch between confirmed, invited, checked-in, and declined guests without losing your place.

![Meridian Gallery portal guest list filtered to confirmed guests](assets/2026-07-29-portals/meridian-portal-guestlist.png)

## Made for wherever you are

The new experience works across desktop and mobile, with your allocations, guest list, inbox, help, and settings always close at hand.

![Meridian Gallery portal allocation view on mobile](assets/2026-07-29-portals/meridian-portal-mobile.png)
