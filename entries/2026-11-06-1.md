[new]

- **Ask for a first and last name.** Turn it on under Settings → Info. Public booking, Add Guests, Invite Guests, personalisation tokens and the name columns in your CSVs then use the two fields.
  ![Invite guests, with separate first name and last name fields](assets/2026-11-06/first-last-name.png "dialog slate")
- **A credit pack comes with an invoice.** You can download it for your records.
- **An email when a new browser signs in.** It includes the method, browser, rough location and time, and a Sign out this device button. Account settings also gain a Devices list, where you can sign out any other browser.
- **A text fallback only goes where it can arrive.** When a WhatsApp invitation cannot be delivered, the text message now only goes out in the UK, Germany, Spain, Latvia and Mexico. Elsewhere no text is sent, and no credit is spent.

[improved]

- **A clearer message when an allocation is full.** A guest opening an invitation or a booking link sees it when an exhibitor's allocation has no places left.
- **Apple Wallet from WhatsApp opens in the phone's browser.** WhatsApp's built-in browser now asks the guest to switch, which is where adding the pass works.
- **The confirmation page says Add guest details.** A booker can see they are filling in names for places they have already booked, rather than adding more people.
- **A promo code ignores capitals and spaces.** It works however a guest types it.
- **A busy card reader cannot be pressed twice.** Staff are told when the reader is still finishing a payment.
- **A failed invitations import says why.** The notice names the reason, for example too many extra columns, and it shows in the new admin too.
- **A stalled invitation batch retries itself.** The invitations-sent email counts only the invitations that actually went out.
- **One WhatsApp failure does not block the number.** A one-off delivery problem no longer marks a guest's number as unreachable for later invitations.
- **Admin search is faster on a short term.** A search of one or two letters comes back sooner.
- **Account emails fit a phone screen.** Downloads, imports and sign-in notices have a cleaner design, including in mobile Gmail.
- **The classic support button points to the new admin.** Live chat is there.

[fixed]

- **An event no longer closes to bookings early.**
- **Saved Advanced settings stay as you left them.** They no longer switch back on their own.
- **The Sales Report CSV includes past bookings.** It no longer comes out empty in the new admin.
- **Net Amount matches the Sales Report.** The Bookings CSV takes off ARTSVP and Stripe fees.
- **Max per booking holds.** It can no longer be got round, including by adding the same ticket on two lines.
- **Adding events to a collection in bulk completes.** It no longer fails part of the way through.
- **Portal contact suggestions work on the Free plan.** A partner on the Free plan gets them again.
- **A portal share email shows the event name.** A tag such as the event name no longer appears as a raw code in the subject of a bulk portal share email.
- **A link preview keeps the image you set.** It no longer reverts to the default image after you replace an event banner.
- **An expired portal list sends you to sign in.** A guest list that never loaded in a tab left open after the session ended now takes you back to the portal's access screen.
- **Booking and Billing stay up in two rare cases.** Joining the ARTSVP Collective mailing list during a booking no longer causes a rare error, and the Billing page loads for an organisation without a subscription.
