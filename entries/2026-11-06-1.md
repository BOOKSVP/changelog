---
title: "First and last names on booking forms, credit pack invoices, and sign-in alerts"
date: 2026-11-06
---

[new]

- Booking forms can ask for First name and Last name separately: turn it on under Settings → Info, and it carries through public booking, Add Guests, Invite Guests, personalisation tokens and the name columns in your CSVs.
- Buying a credit pack now comes with a proper invoice you can download for your records.
- You'll get an email when your account signs in from a new browser, with the method, browser, rough location and time, and a Sign out this device button. Account settings also gain a Devices list where you can sign out any other browser.
- When a WhatsApp invitation can't be delivered, the text-message fallback now only goes out in countries where it can actually arrive (currently the UK, Germany, Spain, Latvia and Mexico). Elsewhere no text is sent and no credit is spent on it.

[improved]

- When an exhibitor's allocation is full, guests opening their invitation or booking link now see a softer, clearer message.
- Opening an Apple Wallet pass from WhatsApp's built-in browser now prompts guests to open it in their phone's browser, where adding the pass works.
- The confirmation page now says Add guest details instead of Add or edit guests, so bookers know they're filling in names for places they've already booked rather than adding more people.
- Promo codes now work however guests type them, in any mix of capitals and with stray spaces.
- Paying on a card reader now tells staff when the reader is still busy finishing a payment, and the button can't be pressed twice.
- When an invitations import fails, the notice now tells you why, for example too many extra columns, and it shows up in the new admin too.
- If sending a batch of invitations stalls, it now retries on its own, and the "invitations sent" email counts only the invitations that actually went out.
- A one-off WhatsApp delivery problem no longer marks a guest's number as unreachable for future invitations.
- Admin search is faster with short search terms.
- Account emails such as downloads, imports and sign-in notices have a cleaner design that fits phone screens, including mobile Gmail.
- The classic admin's support button now points you to the new admin, where live chat is.

[fixed]

- Fixed some events closing to bookings early.
- Fixed saved Advanced settings on an event sometimes switching back on their own.
- Fixed the Sales Report CSV in the new admin coming out empty for past bookings.
- Fixed Net Amount in the Bookings CSV not taking off ARTSVP and Stripe fees, so it now matches the Sales Report.
- Fixed Max per booking on a ticket being possible to get round, including by adding the same ticket on two lines.
- Fixed bulk-adding events to a collection sometimes failing.
- Fixed contact suggestions in portals not working for partners on the Free plan.
- Fixed tags such as the event name showing as raw codes in the subject line of bulk portal share emails.
- Fixed link previews sometimes reverting to the default image after you replaced an event banner.
- Fixed a portal guest list that never loaded in a tab left open after the session ended. It now takes you back to the portal's access screen.
- Fixed a rare error when booking if the guest also joined the ARTSVP Collective mailing list, and the Billing page erroring for organisations without a subscription.
