---
title: "Image uploads in the editor, a simpler free plan, and scanner fixes"
date: 2026-05-11
---

[new]

- Images can now be uploaded directly in rich-text fields — including custom email bodies, event detail pages, and portal messages — and are stored securely rather than embedded as raw data.

[improved]

- New organisations now start on the free plan straight away, removing the demo-plan step and letting you get started faster.
- Contact search is now significantly faster and no longer times out when searching large guest lists.

[fixed]

- Fixed an issue where the CodeREADr scanner integration appeared enabled in settings while scanners were not actually being provisioned.
- Fixed an error that prevented scanner settings from saving when a tag field contained plain text instead of a recognised tag value.
- Fixed pages on custom subdomains occasionally timing out for visitors during periods of high bot traffic.
- Fixed an issue where clicking "Add Card" on a free plan account incorrectly triggered payment processing instead of simply saving the card.
- Fixed a broken link on the credits page.
