title: "Link previews you can edit, portal guest-list downloads, and promo codes on credit packs"
date: 2026-09-28

[new]

- Events and collections get a Social Sharing card: crop a proper link-preview image, edit the title and description, and preview how the link looks in WhatsApp, iMessage, Facebook, LinkedIn and X before you share. Events need Pro or Expert; collections are open to every plan.
- Portal users can download their guest list again — one CSV of the guests and invites in view, with only the details the portal shows.
- Organisations can connect a Google Tag Manager container alongside Meta Pixel, and paid booking pages load configured tracking after cookie consent without switching it on per event.
- Credit pack checkout now lets you add a promo code.

[improved]

- Guest list and door check-in search wait until three characters before running, so short searches no longer freeze large spaces.
- Links in public page and card information text follow your branded text colour.

[fixed]

- Fixed approving a portal access request crashing when the requester already had a cancelled or accepted membership in the organisation.
- Fixed the Notify button returning "page not found" on custom-subdomain sites when attributing a guest who already booked elsewhere.
- Fixed saving event availability timing out when every banner and thumbnail was re-uploaded on each save.
- Fixed the create-event wizard letting you reach Review & Publish when the first draft save had failed.
- Fixed event Overview pages timing out while loading the latest bookings on large events.
- Fixed portal message notification emails linking to the portal home instead of the inbox.
- Fixed guest-list tag filters missing rows when imported tags had trailing spaces.
