---
title: "Configurable credit alerts, customisable invites, and sharper email images"
date: 2026-07-27
---

[new]

- Organisations can now choose which credit and billing alert emails they receive, and set their own threshold for when a "running low on credits" warning fires.
- The call-to-action button in invitation emails is now customisable — event and collection admins can set their own text instead of the fixed "RSVP" or "View Events" label.

[improved]

- Event cards on public collection pages are now clickable anywhere, not just the title, making it easier to open an event from a collection listing.
- Stripe payment receipts and dashboard/CSV exports now show a breakdown of any tax or service charge included in the price.

[fixed]

- Fixed an issue where images attached inside invitation email bodies could look blurry — they now display at the same sharp resolution as banner images.
- Fixed an issue where a signed-in user browsing the public ARTSVP site would still see a "Sign in" button instead of a working sign-out option.
