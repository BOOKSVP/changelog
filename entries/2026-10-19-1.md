---
title: "Scanners that open the right event, and steadier portal invitations"
date: 2026-10-19
summary: "The scanner on a check-in door opens the event on screen, and a portal invitation stays within the places that partner has left."
---

[improved]

- **The Scanner tab opens this event.** On a check-in door it opens the scanner for the event on screen, including an event you have duplicated. That could previously open the original event's scanner.

[fixed]

- **A portal invite respects the places left.** A multi-guest ticket no longer opens the booking form and then fails when the partner's allocation does not have enough places.
- **Add Guests keeps a slot that is underway.** A time slot that has started but has not ended stays available, so you can still add someone to it.
- **The Payouts page shows your Stripe balance.** It no longer fails to load.
- **The tag filter works on an empty list.** An invitations list with nobody on it yet no longer errors.
