---
title: "Scanners that open the right event, and steadier portal invitations"
date: 2026-10-19
---

[improved]

- The Scanner tab on a check-in door now always opens the scanner for the event on screen, including events you've duplicated, which could previously open the original event's scanner.

[fixed]

- Fixed portal invitations for multi-guest tickets opening the booking form and then failing with an error when the partner's allocation didn't have enough places left.
- Fixed Add guest hiding time slots that have already started but haven't ended yet, so you can still add someone to a slot that's in progress.
- Fixed the Payouts page failing to show your Stripe balance.
- Fixed the tag filter erroring on an invitations list with nobody in it yet.
