---
title: "Complimentary guest invites, a smoother new admin, and accurate portal downloads"
date: 2026-08-31
---

[new]

- Invites can now be marked as complimentary, letting specific guests book a paid event for free with no prices shown and no payment step — useful for VIP and member programs.

[improved]

- Low credit balance warning emails now resend after your balance recovers and drops again, instead of staying silent for the rest of the billing cycle.
- Tab navigation and mobile layout are more consistent across Events, Guests, Collections, Portals, and Event Settings in the new admin experience, and Collections and Insights pages load noticeably faster.
- Publishing an event now resumes your draft at the step you left off, and the confirmation clearly states that a private event stays private after publishing.
- Locations, mailing lists, and custom form fields can now be managed directly from Event Settings without leaving the page.
- The Promote page's QR code is larger on mobile, and the credit pack purchase screen now explains what credits are used for.

[fixed]

- Fixed guest list and booking downloads to correctly show which portal a booking came from, including bookings made through a portal's booking links.
- Fixed a crash on some event and collection pages (bookings, links, invites) when arriving via a membership invite link, and corrected live booking/check-in updates that were going to the wrong place.
- Fixed an error when visiting billing or payouts without an organisation selected.
- Fixed tag autocomplete timing out for larger organisations.
- Fixed a display issue where an organisation's custom styling could override the "Powered by ARTSVP" badge on public booking pages.
