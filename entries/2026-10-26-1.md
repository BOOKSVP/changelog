date: 2026-10-26

[improved]

- Uploading a contacts CSV in the new admin now shows a confirmation, so you know the import has started.
- On a phone, the Confirmed tab on a portal guest list stays reachable from the ⋯ menu instead of disappearing when the tabs fold away.

[fixed]

- Fixed fixed-amount promo codes crashing at checkout.
- Fixed some unfinished bookings staying pending forever and holding their places, so those places are released again.
- Fixed an event's end date not updating when you only moved the end of its availability, for example extending a single-slot event's closing date.
- Fixed some older ONE Pass links opening an error page.
- Fixed shared or reloaded links to a guest or invitation in a portal failing to open in the side panel.
- Fixed email delivery and open updates on guest timelines occasionally going missing when several arrived at once.
