---

[improved]

- **A confirmation when a contacts import starts.** Uploading a contacts CSV in the new admin now shows that the import has started.
- **Confirmed stays reachable on a phone.** On a portal guest list, the Confirmed tab stays in the ⋯ menu when the tabs fold away, instead of disappearing.
  ![A portal guest list on a phone, with Confirmed on the tab strip](assets/2026-10-26/portal-guest-list.png "detail olive")

[fixed]

- **A fixed-amount promo code works at checkout.** It no longer crashes.
- **An unfinished booking releases its places.** Some were staying pending forever and holding their places. Those places are released again.
- **An end date follows the availability you move.** Changing only the end of an event's availability, for example extending a single-slot event's closing date, now updates the event's end date.
- **An older ONE Pass link opens.** It no longer opens an error page.
- **A shared portal link opens in the side panel.** A shared or reloaded link to a guest or an invitation in a portal opens again.
- **Timeline updates arrive together.** An email delivery or open update on a guest timeline no longer goes missing when several arrive at once.
