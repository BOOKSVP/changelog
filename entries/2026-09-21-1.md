---
title: "Booking links that work while signed in, invitation reply-to, and fewer duplicate emails"
date: 2026-09-21
---

[new]

- Portal groups can set an Invitation reply-to address, so replies to every exhibitor invitation land in one shared inbox instead of each partner's notification email.

[improved]

- Apple Wallet pass links now open a short confirmation page after the pass is added, so guests know it worked — especially on newer iOS, where the pass can add silently in the background.
- Owning-organisation admins can connect an unconnected portal from Portal Settings, instead of only seeing Continue on the partner welcome screen.
- The public credit calculator can optionally include the monthly subscription price and estimate for fewer than ten portals.
- "Invite guests" is now the primary action on the guest list, so the main next step is clearer.
- Booking pages and the events API stay responsive when many seats are already booked.
- The ticket designer blocks starting a new design when your plan is at its design limit, and disables New Design with a clear notice.

[fixed]

- Fixed public booking, event, and invite links showing "page not found" for anyone already signed in through a portal or ARTSVP account.
- Fixed multi-guest bookings sending duplicate confirmation emails and firing duplicate webhooks.
- Fixed Insights pending-invite counts disagreeing with the guest list Pending tab.
- Fixed booking form fields becoming unreadable on branded pages with light text on a light card.
- Fixed booking confirmation email footers overflowing on phones.
- Fixed the Next button on the post-sign-up create-event step doing nothing, so new organisations can finish onboarding.
- Fixed paid plans falling to Free after a failed card authentication on renewal: the plan stays active while Stripe retries, and billing contacts get emailed with a path to pay the outstanding invoice.
