---
title: "Multiple reminders, smoother collections, and several booking fixes"
date: 2026-05-18
---

[new]

- Events can now have multiple email reminders scheduled — up to three on Pro and Expert plans — so you can send a reminder a week out, the day before, and the morning of, all automatically.
- Organisations can now be deactivated from the settings page, removing them from navigation and preventing further access.

[improved]

- Reorganising events within a collection is now much smoother: drag-and-drop auto-scrolls on long lists, up and down arrows let you nudge items without dragging, and new blocks can be inserted anywhere inline.
- Subscription and credit purchase checkouts now request 3D Secure authentication where your card issuer supports it, adding an extra layer of security to your payments.
- Webhook notifications are now delivered with higher priority, reducing latency when large reminder batches are sending at the same time.
- The mailing list page now loads quickly for organisations with large booking histories, where it previously timed out.

[fixed]

- Fixed an issue where Add to Calendar links showed incorrect end times for bookings in the last slot of the day, overshooting the venue's closing time.
- Fixed an issue where invite links sent from a portal subdomain returned a 404 instead of opening the sign-up page.
- Fixed an issue where the booking form showed "Next" on single-step flows instead of the correct final action label such as "Confirm" or "Join Waitlist".
- Fixed broken plus and minus icons on the ticket quantity control on public booking pages.
- Fixed an issue where booking confirmation pages could time out if the confirmation email was slow to send.
