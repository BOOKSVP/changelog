title: "Tighter portal access, sharper invite images, and fixed collection listings"
date: 2026-08-10
---

[improved]

- Portal group pages now show clearer invite and guest metrics, with new "Invites" and "Guests" columns.

[fixed]

- Fixed an issue where unpublished or archived events could still appear on a collection's public page.
- Fixed an issue where very tall images added to invitation email bodies could still appear blurry — re-add any existing images in the template editor to sharpen them.
- Tightened partner portal access so portal pages and links can only be used by the connected partner organisation.
- Fixed an issue that could cause portal settings updates to fail unexpectedly.
