---
title: "Collection invites that stay on your guest list"
date: 2026-11-09
summary: "A collection invite stays on the guest list until every use is booked, and a booked invite shows as Invited rather than Pending."
---

[improved]

- **A multi-use invite stays until every use is booked.** A collection invite stays on your guest list, with a small use count such as 2/5 next to the name, so you can still re-send it.
- **The Events filter includes collections.** You can narrow the guest list to a collection's own invites, or to everyone booked for its events.
- **A booked invite shows as Invited.** It no longer shows as Pending. Pending counts, and the heads-up that someone is already on the guest list, follow the same rule.
