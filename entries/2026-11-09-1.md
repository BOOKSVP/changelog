---
title: "Collection invites that stay on your guest list"
date: 2026-11-09
---

[improved]

- Multi-use invites, such as collection invites, now stay on your guest list until every use is booked, with a small use count like 2/5 next to the name so you can still re-send them.
- The Events filter on the guest list now includes collections, so you can narrow the list to a collection's own invites or to everyone booked for its events.
- An invite that someone has booked from now shows as Invited rather than Pending, and the Pending counts and the "already on the guest list" heads-up follow the same rule.
