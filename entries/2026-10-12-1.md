---
title: "Free booking links, duplicate-guest heads-ups, and proper booking cancellations"
date: 2026-10-12
---

[new]

- Booking links can skip payment: turn on Skip payment in a link's Advanced settings and anyone with the link books for free, with prices and checkout hidden, and you can set a Max Guests limit if you want to cap places.
- Invite guests and Add guest now give you a heads-up when someone is already invited or booked, even under another of their emails, and contact suggestions flag them, while you can still go ahead or confirm their invitation instead.
- Cancelling a booking now sends a proper cancellation email you can set up per event under Settings → Emails → Cancellation, and you can cancel one booking or many at once from the guest list, with or without an email and an optional message.
- WhatsApp invitations can use your organisation's own approved template, and you can tweak the guest name or event name for a send before it goes.

[improved]

- Choosing an event thumbnail opens a 16:9 cropper and shows the result straight away, and event cards show the whole image.
- Automated invitations now use your saved Invite Email wording instead of ARTSVP's generic text.
- When you cancel an event, emailing Invited and Declined guests is now your choice, each with its own box, while bookers are emailed by default.
- Search in the classic admin is much faster.
- Pages across the app load faster, and the Declined time on guest timelines shows when the guest actually declined.
- Scanners pick up booking, check-in and ONE Pass changes faster and more reliably.
- A collection with no guests now offers Invite Guests instead of New Event.

[fixed]

- Fixed the Sales Report overstating VAT: VAT is summed per payment, only paid bookings count, discounts and refunds come off, and the totals are labelled to match Stripe.
- Fixed invited guests being unable to confirm when a required question was already answered on their invite.
- Fixed Daily Slots events bringing back deleted days on the Availability tab.
- Fixed wallet passes showing HTML codes instead of &, quotes and apostrophes, and long event names being cut off on PDF tickets.
- Fixed card-reader payments sometimes sending guests two confirmation emails.
- Fixed the Powered by ARTSVP footer disappearing on light branded pages.
- Fixed live guest lists erroring after being left open in a background tab.
- Fixed the ONE Pass logo being invisible in light mode on Settings → Info.
- Fixed Instagram links on the website and in emails opening a missing profile.
- Fixed image uploads over 20 MB hanging, so they now get a clear size message instead.
