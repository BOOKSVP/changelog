---
title: "Free booking links, duplicate-guest heads-ups, and proper booking cancellations"
date: 2026-10-12
summary: "Booking links can skip payment, you are warned when someone is already invited, and a cancellation can send an email you write."
---

[new]

- **Skip payment on a booking link.** Turn on Skip payment in a link's Advanced settings and anyone with the link books for free, with prices and checkout hidden. You can set a Max Guests limit if you want to cap places.
- **A heads-up when someone is already invited.** Invite Guests and Add Guests tell you when someone is already invited or booked, even under another of their emails, and contact suggestions flag them. You can still go ahead, or confirm their invitation instead.
- **Cancel a booking with a proper email.** Set the email up per event under Settings → Emails → Cancellation. You can cancel one booking or many at once from the guest list, with or without an email and an optional message.
- **Use your own WhatsApp template.** Invitations can use your organisation's approved template, and you can tweak the guest name or the event name for a send before it goes.

[improved]

- **Event thumbnails crop to 16:9.** Choosing one opens a cropper and shows the result straight away, and event cards show the whole image.
- **Automated invites use your Invite Email.** They use the wording you saved, instead of ARTSVP's generic text.
- **Choose who is emailed when you cancel.** Emailing Invited and Declined guests is now your choice, each with its own box, while bookers are emailed by default.
- **Faster search in the classic admin.** Search there is much faster.
- **Faster pages, and a true Declined time.** Pages across the app load faster, and the Declined time on a guest timeline shows when the guest actually declined.
- **Scanners pick up changes faster.** Booking, check-in and ONE Pass changes arrive more reliably.
- **An empty collection offers Invite Guests.** A collection with no guests offers Invite Guests, instead of New Event.

[fixed]

- **The Sales Report no longer overstates VAT.** VAT is summed per payment, only paid bookings count, and discounts and refunds come off. The totals are labelled to match Stripe.
- **Invited guests can confirm a finished question.** A required question already answered on their invite no longer blocks them from confirming.
- **Daily Slots keep deleted days deleted.** The Availability tab no longer brings back a day you removed.
- **Wallet passes and PDF tickets read correctly.** Passes no longer show HTML codes instead of &, quotes and apostrophes, and long event names are no longer cut off on a PDF ticket.
- **A card-reader payment sends one confirmation.** Guests are no longer sent two confirmation emails.
- **The Powered by ARTSVP footer stays put.** It no longer disappears on a light branded page.
- **A live guest list survives a background tab.** It no longer errors after being left open.
- **The ONE Pass logo shows in light mode.** It is visible again on Settings → Info.
- **Instagram links open the right profile.** Links on the website and in emails no longer open a missing profile.
- **A large image upload says it is too big.** An upload over 20 MB no longer hangs. It gets a clear size message instead.
