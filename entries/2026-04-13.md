---
title: "Cleaner guest access, better passes, and fewer booking headaches"
date: 2026-04-13
---

[improved]

- Exhibitors and event-level team members now only see the bookings, guest lists, and upcoming events that are relevant to their own event, instead of wider collection data.
- Booking confirmation emails now display banner images properly on iPhone Apple Mail, so client comms look more polished on mobile.
- Wallet passes now show the correct single entry time when a timed ticket has a specific entry slot.
- Archived bookings are now kept out of the main all-bookings view, making it easier to focus on current activity.
- Pass fields now strip unwanted formatting tags before appearing in wallet passes, giving guests a cleaner Apple Wallet and Google Wallet experience.

[fixed]

- Fixed an issue that could make invite CSV uploads fail during busy imports.
- Fixed a crash on the event custom fields page caused by broken legacy field links.
- Fixed a guest list error that could happen when someone reached a space view without the right account context.
- Fixed a dashboard issue where event-level users could see bookings and events outside their scope.
