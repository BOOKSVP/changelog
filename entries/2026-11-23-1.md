[new]

- **Refund a guest without leaving ARTSVP.** Organisation admins can send some or all of a card payment back from the booking page, or while cancelling the booking. The money returns to the card it came from.
  ![Refund this booking, with the amount filled in](assets/2026-11-23/refund-dialog.png "dialog gallery")
- **Rename a space from the switcher.** A pencil beside the space opens Rename space. The new name shows in the switcher and across the admin.

[improved]

- **A refund shows beside the status.** Once money has gone back, a small icon sits beside the status, amber for part of the payment and green for all of it, while the status stays Confirmed or Cancelled. Cancelling still does not refund on its own: an organisation admin can tick Refund their payment too, and the cancel confirmation on the public booking page says a refund is not automatic.
- **The admin works on a phone.** Guest lists and other tables become lists you can read, and names and numbers stay whole. Dialogs open from the bottom of the screen, controls are large enough to tap, and a phone no longer zooms in when you tap a form field.
  ![All Guests on a phone, with names and dates in a list](assets/2026-11-23/guest-list-phone.png "phone oak")
- **An event banner fills empty emails.** Uploading an event banner also fills the confirmation and reminder email banners when they are still empty. Social sharing, the image and wording on a shared link, is available on every plan.
- **A switch for Terms of Service.** Under Settings → Info, Terms of Service has an on/off switch: off keeps the text, and booking forms stop asking guests to accept it. The time zone list leads with the city, and Advanced settings can show or hide the image on event cards.
- **A warning before a duplicate organisation.** Choosing Create a new organisation on Connect your portal warns you if a team with that name, or someone at your work email domain, is already on ARTSVP. You can still go ahead by ticking This is a different organisation.
- **Open your portal stays on Connected Portals.** Every connected portal has an Open your portal button, and the page explains that the organiser's events live inside the portal rather than in your Events list.
- **Public pages keep guest details quieter.** A ticket page no longer links to the whole booking, and the booker's email is partly hidden on public booking pages. Thirty days after the booking, those pages show Expired and leave out personal details; the booking stays Confirmed on the guest list, check-in still works, and a signed-in event manager still sees everything.

[fixed]

- **Editing a reminder does not use one up.** Changing a reminder that has already gone out no longer uses up one of the booking's reminders, so a later reminder is not skipped.
- **Door scans stay quick when an email is reopened.** A mail app reopening an email many times no longer slows door scans and payments.
- **Extra guest names and emails are saved.** Names and emails typed for extra guests on a public booking form are saved with the booking.
