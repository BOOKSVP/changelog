---
title: "Per-event invite tabs, a Portals filter, and automatic refunds for late payments"
date: 2026-11-16
summary: "Each event keeps its own invitation wording, All Guests can filter by portal, and a late payment is refunded on its own."
---

[new]

- **One tab per event when you invite across events.** Each tab has that event's own subject, banner and message, so one event's wording never reaches another event's guests.
  ![Invite guests, with a tab for each of three events](assets/2026-11-16/invite-tabs.png "dialog cobalt")
- **A Portals filter on All Guests.** Under Booking links, show only the guests a partner portal brought in, for one portal or several, alongside your other filters.
  ![The Portals filter, with two partner portals selected](assets/2026-11-16/portals-filter.png "detail ochre")

[improved]

- **Select all starts fresh on a filtered list.** It replaces your earlier ticks, and an "N hidden" badge warns you when the selection includes guests you cannot currently see.
- **A partner's own invites stay on their portal.** An invitation a partner sends from their portal no longer appears on your All Guests list. Anyone who books through one still shows on your list, with the portal's name.
- **An API response shows your rate limit.** Every response shows the limit and how much is left. You get an email at 80% of the limit and if calls are paused, and a paused call tells you when to try again.
- **The classic admin counts down to 1 December.** Its banner shows the days until it closes, with a Switch now button and a Questions? link.

[fixed]

- **A late payment no longer charges the guest.** When a booking hold ends, the unfinished payment is cancelled, and any payment that still gets through is refunded automatically.
- **The API rate limit resets when it should.** An organisation is no longer kept locked out long after the limit should have cleared.
