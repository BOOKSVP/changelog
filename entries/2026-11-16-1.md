---
title: "Per-event invite tabs, a Portals filter, and automatic refunds for late payments"
date: 2026-11-16
---

[new]

- Sending invitations to guests across several events now opens one tab per event, each with that event's own subject, banner and message, so one event's wording can never reach another event's guests.
- All Guests has a new Portals filter under Booking links, so you can show just the guests a partner portal brought in, for one portal or several, alongside your other filters.

[improved]

- Select all on a filtered guest list now replaces your earlier ticks instead of adding to them, and an "N hidden" badge warns you when your selection includes guests you can't currently see.
- Invitations a partner sends from their own portal now stay with that portal and no longer appear on your All Guests list, while anyone who books through one shows on your list as normal with the portal's name.
- If you use the ARTSVP API, every response now shows your rate limit and how much is left, you'll get an email at 80% of the limit and if calls are paused, and a paused call tells you when to try again.
- The classic admin's banner now counts down the days until it closes on 1 December, with a Switch now button and a Questions? link.

[fixed]

- Fixed guests occasionally being charged after their booking hold had run out: an unfinished payment is now cancelled when the hold ends, and any payment that still gets through is refunded automatically.
- Fixed the API rate limit sometimes keeping an organisation locked out long after it should have reset.
