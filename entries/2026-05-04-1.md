---
title: "Social links on confirmations, smarter portal reports, and invite fixes"
date: 2026-05-04
---

[new]

- Organisations can now add social media links (Instagram, X, TikTok, Facebook, and website) in their settings; these appear automatically on booking confirmation pages so guests can follow along after their visit.
- More credit pack sizes — 100, 250, 500, 1,000, and 2,500 credits — are now available to purchase.

[improved]

- Portal group CSV exports now include link conversion percentages and allocation totals, making it straightforward to see how booking links are performing across exhibitors.
- Product prices and descriptions on booking forms now follow your organisation's custom brand colours instead of always appearing in grey.
- The billing page no longer lists void invoices, keeping billing history cleaner.

[fixed]

- Fixed an issue where an invite could be declined after a booking had already been made, leaving the invite and booking in conflicting states.
- Fixed an issue where unchecking individual invites after "Select All" didn't update the selected count, so bulk actions would apply to the full list.
- Fixed an issue where Apple Wallet membership card passes could fail to generate for returning passholders.
- Fixed an issue where organisations switching to an annual plan had their next monthly credit refresh incorrectly set to the end of their billing year rather than one month out.
- Fixed an issue where the billing page would fail to load for some accounts.
- Fixed an issue where scanner codes could fail to sync under concurrent load, temporarily affecting door check-in.
- Fixed an issue where searching bookings by date range for a space could return an error.
