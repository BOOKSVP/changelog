---
title: "Smoother organisation switching, steadier webhooks, and safer imports"
date: 2026-08-24
---

[fixed]

- Fixed an issue where switching away from a restricted (on-hold) organisation could leave you stuck, with the Back button and other navigation returning you to the on-hold page.
- Fixed an issue where retried webhook deliveries could get stuck and never complete, and where a single successful delivery could mask ongoing failures on that endpoint.
- Fixed an issue where a booking import with invalid data could create a booking with an unreasonably large guest count.
