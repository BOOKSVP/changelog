---
title: "Smarter booking forms, better invite tracking, and billing fixes"
date: 2026-04-27
---

[new]

- Custom fields on booking forms can now include a description that appears beneath the field label, giving guests helpful context when filling in their details.

[improved]

- Portal CSV exports now include invite utilisation percentages, showing how much of each exhibitor's invite allocation has been sent and how much has been redeemed.

[fixed]

- Fixed an issue where organisations on a legacy plan could not access the Collections area and were shown a 404 error.
- Fixed a bug where entering guest details at booking time did not correctly count those guests against the allocation.
- Fixed the billing page displaying subscription prices in the wrong currency.
- Fixed discount coupon codes that were not being applied correctly.
