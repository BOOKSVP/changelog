[new]

- **Cancel an event in three steps.** Choose whether to email guests, write the message, and confirm. Cancelled events read as cancelled everywhere: booking pages, tickets with the QR greyed out, ONE Pass, portals and the public feed. Invited guests with an email are told too, not only bookers, and scheduled reminders stop.
  ![Cancel this event, choosing whether to email guests](assets/2026-10-05/cancel-event.png "dialog marble")
- **WhatsApp invites open with a picture.** The message starts with the event's picture, the same Social Sharing card used for link previews, so guests see the show before they tap through.
  ![A WhatsApp invitation opening with the event picture](assets/2026-10-05/whatsapp-invite.png "detail rose")
- **Several emails can belong to one person.** Merge them on a contact card so guest history reads as one profile. Guests can add emails under Your emails on ONE Pass, and the pass then opens bookings for every linked address.
  ![Merge contacts, with a second email ready to join the same person](assets/2026-10-05/merge-contacts.png "dialog mist")
- **Start from a public event, with a real venue.** Creating an event offers a Public Event starting point. New organisations set a real venue, or Online, during onboarding, so wallet passes work from the first event.
- **Filter a guest list by booking link.** Guest lists gain a Booking links filter, and the Guests count on a link panel opens that filtered list, so you can see who booked through a specific link without a CSV.
- **Turn on a Created column.** On a portal, All Guests, an event or a collection guest list, it sorts newest first when you turn it on.

[improved]

- **Removing an event keeps that partner's guests.** Their invitations and booking links stay on the portal. You choose whether what is already sent can still be used, or force-close the allocation.
- **Google Wallet shows a minting page.** Add to Wallet waits on that page while the pass generates, instead of opening a blank tab.
- **Clearer copy across the new admin.** Tooltips, empty states, hints and confirmations are rewritten as plain whole sentences.

[fixed]

- **Form pop-ups no longer vanish on an error.** When something went wrong they left a blank screen and lost what you had typed. They now stay open and tell you what happened.
- **Apple Wallet links open the pass.** They no longer send a guest into organiser onboarding when they happen to be signed in with an unfinished account.
- **Allow access emails open the accept page.** A portal owner is no longer sent to create-your-first-event instead.
- **The last extra or promo code saves.** Removing it no longer fails, so the row does not come back after you save.
- **Heavy guest lists no longer time out.** Contact autocomplete, and invite and guest-list pages on the heaviest organisations, load again.
